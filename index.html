FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you bring your own supplies and equipment?
Yes — we arrive fully equipped with industrial-grade HEPA vacuums, eco-friendly cleaning solutions, and specialty tools. You don't need to provide anything.
Are you licensed and insured?
Absolutely. We carry full general liability insurance and are fully licensed for both residential and commercial properties throughout LA, Orange, and Ventura Counties.
What cities do you serve?
We serve Los Angeles, Santa Monica, Beverly Hills, Malibu, Venice, Hollywood, Studio City, Calabasas, Sherman Oaks, Encino, Tarzana, Woodland Hills, West Hollywood, and surrounding areas.
Do you offer same-day service?
Yes. Call us directly at (213) 310-3848 for urgent requests. We are available day and night throughout Los Angeles.
How much does post-construction cleaning cost in Los Angeles?
Every job is different based on square footage and condition. Most post-construction cleans range from $300-$1,500. We offer free estimates with a response within 2 hours. Call (213) 310-3848 or fill out our form.
How long does a post-construction clean take?
A standard post-construction clean takes 4-8 hours depending on property size. Large commercial builds may take 2 days. We work around your inspection and handoff timeline.
Do you work directly with general contractors?
Yes. We work directly with GCs, developers, and project managers throughout LA County. We understand construction timelines and can be on site within 24 hours of project completion.
What is included in a deep clean vs a regular clean?
A regular clean covers surfaces, floors, bathrooms and kitchen basics. A deep clean goes further — inside appliances, cabinet interiors, grout lines, baseboards, window tracks, and behind furniture. Recommended every 3-6 months.
